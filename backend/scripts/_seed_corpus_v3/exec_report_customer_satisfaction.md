# Report: Customer Satisfaction and Quality Metrics — Q2 2026

**Document ID:** GEN-EXEC-CSAT-2026-Q2 | **Filename:** exec_report_customer_satisfaction.md | **Department:** Executive
**Document Type:** Report | **Version:** 1.0 | **Effective Date:** 2026-07-05
**Owner:** VP of Operations | **Access Classification:** Internal

## OVERVIEW

Quarterly customer satisfaction and product-quality metrics, compiled from the quality inspection
program (SOP-MFG-102) and customer complaint intake.

## KEY METRICS

- Customer satisfaction score: 4.3 / 5.0 (up from 4.1 in Q1)
- Product return rate: 0.8% (down from 1.1% in Q1)
- Quality inspection pass rate (SOP-MFG-102): 98.6%
- Average complaint resolution time: 3.2 business days

## COMPLAINT THEMES

The largest complaint category (41% of all Q2 complaints) was packaging seal integrity, which
correlates with the Line 7 stoppage and conveyor incidents earlier in the quarter
(GEN-INC-MFG-2026-014, GEN-INC-ENG-2026-009) — both events are flagged as contributing factors in
the root-cause review, though neither incident alone accounts for the full complaint volume.

## ACTIONS TAKEN

Quality inspection frequency at the Line 7 packaging cell was increased from hourly to
every-30-minutes spot checks for the remainder of Q2, per an addendum to SOP-MFG-102. This is
credited with the return-rate improvement between the two halves of the quarter.

## OUTLOOK

Q3 target: hold customer satisfaction at or above 4.3 and reduce packaging-seal complaints below
30% of total complaint volume, contingent on the CV-350 spare-parts inventory rebuild
(GEN-EXEC-FIN-2026) preventing a repeat of Q2's conveyor-driven downtime.
