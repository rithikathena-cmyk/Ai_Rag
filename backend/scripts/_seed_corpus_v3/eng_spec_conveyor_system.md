# Specification: CV-350 Conveyor System

**Owner:** Engineering Manager | **Access Classification:** Internal

## OVERVIEW

The CV-350 belt conveyor feeds finished cases from the Line 7 packaging cell to the palletizing
station. Installed 2025-09, manufacturer Meridian Handling Systems.

## SPECIFICATIONS

- Belt speed: variable, 0.3-1.2 m/s, controlled from the Line 7 HMI
- Load capacity: 40 kg per linear meter
- Belt material: food-grade PVC, FDA-compliant
- Drive: 2.2 kW variable-frequency motor, mounted at the palletizing end
- Length: 18 meters, three straight sections plus one 90-degree transfer curve

## MAINTENANCE INTERVALS

Belt tension check: weekly. Drive motor bearing lubrication: every 500 operating hours, tracked in
the CMMS alongside the FX-2200's own preventive maintenance schedule (ENG-PM-2026). Belt tracking
alignment: monthly, or immediately if product is observed drifting toward either edge.

## KNOWN FAILURE MODES

Belt mistracking is the most common fault, usually caused by product buildup on the idler rollers
near the transfer curve — addressed by the weekly cleaning step in the standard shift-end checklist.
A full belt replacement is estimated at 6 hours of downtime and requires a spare belt kept in the
maintenance shop inventory at all times (minimum stock: 1 unit).
