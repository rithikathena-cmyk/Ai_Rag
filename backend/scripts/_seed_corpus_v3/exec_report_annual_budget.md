# Report: Annual Budget Summary — FY2026

**Document ID:** GEN-EXEC-FIN-2026 | **Filename:** exec_report_annual_budget.md | **Department:** Executive
**Document Type:** Financial Report | **Version:** 1.0 | **Effective Date:** 2026-01-20
**Owner:** CFO | **Access Classification:** Restricted

## OVERVIEW

Consolidated FY2026 operating budget across Manufacturing, Engineering, and HR cost centers,
approved by the executive team following the Q4 FY2025 planning cycle referenced in
GEN-EXEC-STRAT-2026 (Strategic Manufacturing Plan).

## BUDGET BY COST CENTER (USD, thousands)

| Cost Center | FY2025 Actual | FY2026 Budget | Change |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Manufacturing operations | 4,820 | 5,100 | +5.8% |
| Engineering / maintenance capital | 1,140 | 1,450 | +27.2% |
| HR / workforce programs | 680 | 720 | +5.9% |
| Facilities & EHS | 390 | 460 | +17.9% |
| **Total** | **7,030** | **7,730** | **+10.0%** |

## NOTABLE INCREASES

Engineering's capital budget increase is primarily the CV-350 spare-parts inventory buildout
(following the June conveyor incident, GEN-INC-ENG-2026-009) and a planned second preventive-
maintenance technician headcount. Facilities & EHS growth reflects expanded PPE stocking (EHS-
PPE-201) and the LOTO training program rollout (EHS-SAFE-005).

## CONTINGENCY

A 5% contingency reserve (386K) is held centrally, released only with CFO and CEO joint sign-off,
for unplanned capital needs such as an unplanned equipment failure beyond the CV-350 spare
inventory's coverage.
