# Policy: New Hire Onboarding

**Document ID:** HR-POL-110 | **Filename:** hr_policy_onboarding.md | **Department:** HR
**Document Type:** Policy | **Version:** 2.0 | **Effective Date:** 2026-01-05
**Owner:** VP of Human Resources | **Access Classification:** Internal

## OVERVIEW

Every new hire completes a structured 2-week onboarding regardless of department, coordinated by
HR with the hiring manager.

## WEEK ONE

**Day 1:** HR orientation — benefits enrollment (see HR-BEN-100), IT account setup, badge issuance,
and a review of HR-POL-201 (Code of Conduct). New hires sign an acknowledgment of HR-POL-201 before
receiving system access.

**Day 2-3:** Department-specific safety orientation. Manufacturing new hires additionally complete
PPE fitting (EHS-PPE-201) and a supervised floor walkthrough before being permitted on the
production floor unsupervised.

**Day 4-5:** Role-specific training assigned by the hiring manager; shadowing an experienced team
member is the default format unless the role has a formal training track (e.g. LOTO certification
for maintenance hires, per EHS-SAFE-005).

## WEEK TWO

Continued role ramp-up, with a check-in between the new hire and their manager at the end of the
first week and again at the end of the second. HR sends a 30-day and 90-day pulse survey
automatically; a manager who receives a concerning 30-day response should escalate to HR within 48
hours rather than wait for the 90-day survey.

## PROBATIONARY PERIOD

All new hires are on a 90-day probationary period. Performance concerns during this window should
be documented and discussed with HR before the 90-day mark — extending probation requires HR
approval and is communicated to the employee in writing.
