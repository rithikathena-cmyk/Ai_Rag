# Manual: Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) Requirements

**Document ID:** EHS-PPE-201 | **Filename:** mfg_manual_ppe_requirements.md | **Department:** Manufacturing
**Document Type:** Manual | **Version:** 2.1 | **Effective Date:** 2026-01-15
**Owner:** EHS Manager | **Access Classification:** Internal

## SCOPE

Applies to all personnel and visitors entering the production floor, including Line 7 and the
maintenance shop.

## REQUIRED PPE BY ZONE

**Production floor (general):** Safety glasses, closed-toe non-slip shoes, hearing protection in
posted high-noise zones (above 85 dB, marked with yellow signage near Line 7's compressor bank).

**Line 7 filling area:** All general-floor PPE plus a face shield when the FX-2200's fill head
cover is open, and chemical-resistant gloves when handling the product concentrate before dilution.

**Maintenance / LOTO work:** All general-floor PPE plus cut-resistant gloves and, for hydraulic
line work, a face shield rated for high-pressure fluid injection risk.

**Visitors and office staff on a floor walk:** Safety glasses and closed-toe shoes are mandatory;
hearing protection is required only in posted zones.

## INSPECTION AND REPLACEMENT

Safety glasses and face shields are inspected for scratches or cracks at the start of each shift —
damaged PPE must be replaced immediately from the PPE station near the Line 7 entrance, not repaired
or taped. Gloves are single-shift-use for chemical-resistant types; cut-resistant gloves are
inspected weekly by the Shift Lead and replaced at the first sign of fraying.

## NON-COMPLIANCE

A Shift Lead who observes a PPE violation stops the affected work immediately. Repeated violations
follow the same escalation path as other safety violations under HR-POL-201 (Code of Conduct).
