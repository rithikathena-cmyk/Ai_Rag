# SOP: Lockout/Tagout (LOTO) Procedure

**Document ID:** EHS-SAFE-005 | **Filename:** mfg_sop_lockout_tagout.md | **Department:** Manufacturing
**Document Type:** SOP | **Version:** 3.0 | **Effective Date:** 2026-02-01
**Owner:** EHS Manager | **Access Classification:** Internal

## PURPOSE

Establishes the required steps for isolating and de-energizing production equipment before
maintenance, cleaning, or clearing a jam — referenced by SOP-MFG-104 (Machine Shutdown) and
SOP-MFG-101 (Line 7 Operation) as the mandatory de-energization procedure whenever maintenance
access is required.

## WHO MUST FOLLOW THIS PROCEDURE

Any employee performing maintenance, repair, cleaning, or clearing of a jam on a production line
machine. Operators may assist but may not perform the isolation steps themselves unless certified
as an Authorized Employee per the annual LOTO training program.

## LOCKOUT STEPS

1. Notify affected operators that the machine is going down for lockout.
2. Identify all energy sources for the machine: electrical, pneumatic, and hydraulic. The FX-2200
   filling machine has three isolation points — main disconnect, air supply valve, hydraulic shutoff.
3. Shut down the machine using its normal stop procedure.
4. Isolate each energy source at its disconnect point.
5. Apply a lock and tag to each isolation point. Each Authorized Employee uses their own
   individually-keyed lock — no shared or master keys on the production floor.
6. Release stored energy (bleed air lines, relieve hydraulic pressure, discharge capacitors) before
   beginning work.
7. Verify isolation by attempting a normal start — the machine must not start. Return controls to
   neutral/off immediately after verifying.

## RESTORING TO SERVICE

1. Confirm all tools have been removed and guards reinstalled.
2. Confirm all personnel are clear of the machine.
3. Remove locks and tags — only the employee who applied a lock may remove it. If that employee is
   unavailable, removal requires Shift Lead authorization and a documented attempt to contact them.
4. Notify affected operators before re-energizing.
5. Re-energize and test-run before returning to full production.

## TRAINING AND AUTHORIZATION

Authorized Employee status requires the annual 4-hour LOTO training and a practical sign-off from
the EHS Manager, logged in the training system. Untrained employees may not apply or remove a
lock under any circumstance, including during an emergency shutdown response.

## VIOLATIONS

Bypassing a lockout, using another employee's lock, or restarting a locked-out machine without
authorization is treated as a serious safety violation subject to immediate corrective action per
HR-POL-201 (Code of Conduct).
