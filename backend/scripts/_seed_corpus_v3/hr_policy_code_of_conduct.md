# Policy: Code of Conduct

**Document ID:** HR-POL-201 | **Filename:** hr_policy_code_of_conduct.md | **Department:** HR
**Document Type:** Policy | **Version:** 5.0 | **Effective Date:** 2026-01-01
**Owner:** VP of Human Resources | **Access Classification:** Internal

## PURPOSE

Sets the baseline standard of professional conduct for every employee, referenced throughout this
handbook (onboarding acknowledgment per HR-POL-110, safety violation escalation per EHS-SAFE-005
and EHS-PPE-201) as the governing document for conduct-related corrective action.

## WORKPLACE CONDUCT

Employees are expected to treat coworkers, vendors, and visitors with professionalism and respect.
Harassment, discrimination, and retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith are
prohibited and are grounds for immediate corrective action up to termination, following the process
in HR-INC-210 (Grievance Incident Handling).

## SAFETY COMPLIANCE

Compliance with EHS procedures (EHS-SAFE-005, EHS-PPE-201, and department-specific SOPs) is a
condition of employment, not optional guidance. Willful bypass of a safety control — such as
overriding a lockout or working without required PPE — is treated as a serious violation regardless
of whether an incident actually occurred.

## CONFIDENTIALITY

Employees with access to confidential or restricted-classification information (per each
document's own Access Classification field) may not share it outside their authorized role or
department without approval from the document owner or their manager.

## CORRECTIVE ACTION LADDER

1. Verbal coaching, documented by the manager.
2. Written warning, copied to HR.
3. Final written warning with a defined improvement period.
4. Termination.

A serious first-time violation (safety, harassment, confidentiality breach) may skip directly to
step 3 or 4 at HR's discretion — the ladder is a default sequence, not a guaranteed number of
chances.

## REPORTING A CONCERN

Employees may report a concern to their manager, HR directly, or via the anonymous ethics line
(extension 4900). Reports are handled per HR-INC-210's confidentiality provisions.
