# Quarterly Operations Summary — Q1 2026

**Document ID:** EXEC-SUM-202 | **Filename:** exec_summary_quarterly_operations.md | **Department:** Executive
**Document Type:** Report | **Version:** 1.0 | **Effective Date:** 2026-03-31
**Owner:** Chief Operating Officer | **Access Classification:** Internal

## OVERVIEW

This summary consolidates production, quality, and workforce trends for Q1 2026 for executive
review.

## PRODUCTION VOLUME

Total production volume across all lines was up **7.1% quarter-over-quarter**, driven by strong
demand and the Line 7 rate improvements introduced in SOP-MFG-101's current revision.

## OPERATIONAL HIGHLIGHTS

- Enterprise OEE: 79.4% (see EXEC-RPT-201 Plant Performance Report for full detail).
- Two notable incidents this quarter (Line 7 stoppage, Line 4 hydraulic leak) with no injuries
  beyond one minor first-aid case; both closed with corrective actions in the preventive
  maintenance schedule.
- Recruitment activity remained steady; see HR-SOP-201 for the current recruitment process.

## COST AND EFFICIENCY

Maintenance labor efficiency improved slightly following the CNC predictive-maintenance pilot on
Line 7 (a separate initiative referenced in prior-quarter engineering reporting); a capital
request for wider rollout is under review by the finance committee, referenced in the Strategic
Manufacturing Plan (EXEC-PLAN-301).

## WORKFORCE

Headcount and attrition trends are reported through HR channels; attendance specifically is
tracked in the Attendance KPI Summary (EXEC-KPI-101), which is the authoritative source for
enterprise attendance figures — this summary does not restate that detail.

## RISK NOTES

No material new enterprise risks were identified this quarter beyond the ongoing preventive
maintenance program expansion tracked in ENG-PM-2026 and the ordinary course of the HR grievance
process (case volumes remain within normal range; case-level detail is restricted to HR
Compliance).

## OUTLOOK

Management expects continued volume growth into Q2, contingent on the preventive maintenance
program closing the OEE gap identified in the Plant Performance Report.
