# Plant Performance Report — Q1 2026

**Document ID:** EXEC-RPT-201 | **Filename:** exec_report_plant_performance.md | **Department:** Executive
**Document Type:** Report | **Version:** 1.0 | **Effective Date:** 2026-03-31
**Owner:** Plant Manager | **Access Classification:** Internal

## OVERVIEW

This report summarizes plant-wide manufacturing performance for Q1 2026 across all production
lines.

## OVERALL EQUIPMENT EFFECTIVENESS

Enterprise-wide OEE for Q1 2026 was **79.4%**, slightly below the 82% target used by individual
lines (e.g. Line 7's target per SOP-MFG-101). The gap is attributed primarily to the Line 7
unplanned stoppage in February (see INC-MFG-2026-014, 3 hours 42 minutes, root cause a conveyor
bearing failure) and the Line 4 hydraulic leak event (INC-ENG-2026-005).

## SAFETY PERFORMANCE

The Q1 safety incident rate was **0.8 recordable incidents per 100 employees**, within the annual
target range. The Line 7 stoppage incident included one minor first-aid case; the Line 4 hydraulic
leak resulted in no injuries.

## QUALITY PERFORMANCE

Customer-reported quality escapes remained low across all lines, consistent with the AQL 0.65
sampling standard applied on Line 7 (WI-QA-101) and comparable standards on other lines.

## MAINTENANCE PROGRAM UPDATE

Following the Q1 incidents, Engineering updated the preventive maintenance schedule to add the
outfeed conveyor bearing to Line 7's quarterly checklist (ENG-PM-2026) and is reviewing crimped
hydraulic fittings plant-wide.

## WORKFORCE NOTE

Manufacturing attendance metrics for Q1 are reported separately in the Attendance KPI Summary
(EXEC-KPI-101); this report does not duplicate that detail.

## OUTLOOK

Barring further unplanned stoppages, Engineering and Manufacturing project OEE recovery to the
82% target range by Q2 2026, supported by the expanded preventive maintenance checklist.
