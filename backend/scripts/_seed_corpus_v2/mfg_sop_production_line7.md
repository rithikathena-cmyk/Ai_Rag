# SOP: Production Line 7 Filling and Packaging Operation

**Document ID:** SOP-MFG-101 | **Filename:** mfg_sop_production_line7.md | **Department:** Manufacturing
**Document Type:** SOP | **Version:** 2.1 | **Effective Date:** 2026-01-20
**Owner:** Manufacturing Operations Manager | **Access Classification:** Internal

## PURPOSE

This SOP defines the standard operating steps for running Production Line 7, which fills and
packages liquid product into 2-liter containers using the FX-2200 filling machine (see
ENG-SPEC-210 for the machine specification).

## TARGET PERFORMANCE

Line 7's standard rate is **42 units per minute** at full speed, with a target Overall Equipment
Effectiveness (OEE) of **82%**. Any shift averaging below 75% OEE must be logged in the shift
handoff report with a stated root cause.

## STARTUP SEQUENCE

1. Confirm the previous shift's handoff report shows no open quality holds on Line 7.
2. Verify product tank level is above the 20% low-level sensor before starting the fill pump.
3. Run 10 units in manual mode and inspect fill volume against the target of 2.00L ± 0.02L.
4. Once 10 consecutive units pass, switch to automatic mode and ramp to full line speed over 5
   minutes.

## IN-PROCESS CHECKS

- Fill volume is spot-checked every 30 minutes per WI-QA-101 (In-Process Quality Inspection).
- Seal integrity is checked every hour using the pull-test fixture; minimum acceptable seal
  strength is 12N (see WI-QA-101 for the full test procedure).
- Line speed deviations greater than 10% from the 42 units/minute target for more than 15 minutes
  must be reported to the Shift Lead.

## SHUTDOWN AND HANDOFF

At end of shift, complete the Line 7 handoff report including current OEE, any quality holds, and
remaining product tank level. See SOP-MFG-104 (Machine Shutdown Procedure) for full shutdown steps
if the line is stopping for more than 4 hours.
