# Equipment Maintenance Manual — Line 7 Filling Machine FX-2200

**Document Type:** Maintenance Manual | **Version:** 2.3 | **Effective Date:** 2026-01-18
**Owner:** Engineering Manager | **Access Classification:** Internal

## PURPOSE

This manual covers routine maintenance procedures for the FX-2200 filling machine on Production
Line 7 (see ENG-SPEC-210 for the full machine specification).

## LUBRICATION SCHEDULE

Main drive gearbox: check oil level weekly, full oil change every 2,000 operating hours using
ISO VG 220 gear oil. Fill-head actuator rails: grease every 500 operating hours with the
lithium-complex grease specified on the machine's lubrication chart.

## BEARING REPLACEMENT

The outfeed conveyor bearing and the main drive shaft bearings are rated for **4,000 operating
hours**. Replace proactively at this interval rather than waiting for failure — see incident report
INC-MFG-2026-014, in which a bearing that missed this interval caused a 3-hour-42-minute line
stoppage.

## FILL VALVE MAINTENANCE

Inspect fill valve seals monthly for wear; replace at the first sign of drip-through during the
seal integrity check (see WI-QA-101). Fill valve seal kits are stocked in the Line 7 spares crib,
part number FX2200-SEAL-KIT-04.

## SENSOR CALIBRATION

The jam-detect sensor and the volumetric fill sensor must be calibrated quarterly against the
certified reference standard. Calibration records are logged in the CMMS against the machine's
asset tag.

## TROUBLESHOOTING QUICK REFERENCE

- Repeated jam-detect false trips: check outfeed conveyor bearing wear first (most common cause).
- Fill volume drifting high: check fill valve seal wear.
- Line speed unable to reach 42 units/minute: check main drive gearbox oil level and drive belt
  tension.

## SAFETY

All maintenance requiring guard removal follows lockout/tagout per EHS-SAFE-005. Hot-component
work (fill nozzles, seal heads) requires the 15-minute cooldown specified in SOP-MFG-104.
