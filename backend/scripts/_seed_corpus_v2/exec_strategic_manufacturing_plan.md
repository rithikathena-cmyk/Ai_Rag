# Strategic Manufacturing Plan 2026–2028

**Document ID:** EXEC-PLAN-301 | **Filename:** exec_strategic_manufacturing_plan.md | **Department:** Executive
**Document Type:** Strategic Plan | **Version:** 1.0 | **Effective Date:** 2026-03-15
**Owner:** Chief Operating Officer | **Access Classification:** Restricted

## PURPOSE

This plan sets the three-year strategic direction for manufacturing capital investment, capacity,
and operational excellence, for board and executive review.

## STRATEGIC OBJECTIVES

1. Raise enterprise OEE from the current 79.4% (Q1 2026, see EXEC-RPT-201) to a target of **85% by
   2028**.
2. Expand predictive-maintenance coverage from the Line 7 pilot to all filling and packaging
   lines.
3. Reduce unplanned-downtime incidents (see INC-MFG-2026-014 and INC-ENG-2026-005 as the Q1 2026
   baseline examples) by 50% by 2027 through the expanded preventive maintenance program
   (ENG-PM-2026).

## CAPITAL PLAN

A **$4.2 million** capital request is planned for FY2027 to fund predictive-maintenance
sensor rollout across Lines 3–8, contingent on board finance committee approval following the
CNC pilot results referenced in prior engineering reporting.

## WORKFORCE STRATEGY

Attendance and retention trends (see EXEC-KPI-101) will continue to be monitored as an input to
workforce planning, particularly for skilled maintenance technician roles whose availability
gates the pace of the preventive-maintenance expansion.

## RISK CONSIDERATIONS

Execution risk centers on equipment lead times and vendor concentration for the predictive
maintenance sensor platform (single-sourced as of this plan's effective date), and on maintaining
current safety and quality performance while capital projects are underway.

## GOVERNANCE

Progress against this plan is reviewed quarterly alongside the Plant Performance Report and
Quarterly Operations Summary, with a formal annual refresh each March.

## CONFIDENTIALITY

This plan contains forward-looking capital and strategic information and is restricted to
executive leadership and the board; it is not distributed to department-level management outside
executive briefings.
