# HR Policy: Employee Leave and Attendance Policy

Document ID: HR-POL-007 | Revision: 4 | Effective Date: 2026-01-01 | Owner: Human Resources

## PURPOSE

This policy establishes the rules for requesting, approving, and tracking employee leave and
attendance across all departments. It applies to all full-time and part-time employees, excluding
contractors engaged through a staffing agency.

## LEAVE CATEGORIES

1. **Paid Time Off (PTO):** Full-time employees accrue 1.25 days of PTO per completed month of
   service, capped at 20 days per calendar year. PTO is prorated for employees hired mid-month.
2. **Sick Leave:** 8 days per calendar year, non-carryover. A doctor's note is required for any
   sick leave exceeding 3 consecutive days.
3. **Bereavement Leave:** Up to 5 paid days for immediate family, 2 paid days for extended family,
   as defined in Appendix A of the Employee Handbook.
4. **Parental Leave:** 12 weeks of job-protected leave, of which the first 6 weeks are paid at
   100% base salary. Employees must provide at least 30 days' notice where the leave is
   foreseeable.

## REQUESTING LEAVE

All leave requests must be submitted through the HR portal at least 5 business days in advance for
planned leave. Emergency leave should be reported to the employee's direct manager as soon as
possible, with the portal entry completed within 2 business days of return.

## ATTENDANCE TRACKING

Employees clock in and out using the badge terminal at each facility entrance. Three or more
unexcused absences within a rolling 90-day period trigger an automatic notification to the
employee's manager and an HR case file. Chronic attendance issues are handled per the progressive
discipline steps in Section 6 of the Employee Handbook.

## APPROVAL WORKFLOW

Direct managers approve or deny PTO and sick leave requests within 2 business days. Parental leave
and bereavement leave requests are routed to HR directly for processing alongside the manager
notification. Denials must include a documented business reason and are subject to appeal through
the HR Business Partner.

## REMOTE AND HYBRID ATTENDANCE

Employees on an approved hybrid schedule must log their in-office days in the portal by end of day
Friday for the following week. Failure to log a required in-office day is treated as an unexcused
absence for attendance-tracking purposes only, not for pay.

## LEAVE BALANCE INQUIRIES AND DISPUTES

Employees can view current PTO and sick leave balances in the HR portal under "My Time Off." For
balance discrepancies, contact HR Operations at hr-ops@examplecorp.internal or by phone at
(312) 555-0199. Disputes must be raised within 30 days of the pay period in question.
