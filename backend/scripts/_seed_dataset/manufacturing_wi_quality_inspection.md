# Work Instruction: Quality Inspection Checklist for Extruded Aluminum Parts

Document ID: WI-QA-014 | Revision: 5 | Effective Date: 2026-02-01 | Owner: Quality Assurance

## PURPOSE

This work instruction describes the dimensional and visual inspection steps Quality Inspectors
must perform on extruded aluminum profiles before release to the packaging line.

## REQUIRED TOOLS

Digital calipers (0.01mm resolution), go/no-go gauge set (part-specific, stored in QA cabinet 3),
surface comparator plate, and the current inspection tablet running the QMS app.

## SAMPLING PLAN

Use the AQL 1.0 sampling table posted in the QA office for lot sizes under 5,000 units. For
first-article approval on a new extrusion die, inspect 100% of the first production run of 25
pieces before releasing to normal sampling.

## VISUAL INSPECTION CRITERIA

1. **Surface finish:** No visible die lines deeper than 0.05mm, no oxidation streaking, and no
   pickup marks longer than 10mm. Compare against the reference sample in the surface comparator
   kit.
2. **Cut ends:** Both ends must be square within 1 degree and free of burrs that catch a
   fingernail test.
3. **Straightness:** Bow must not exceed 1mm per meter of length when checked on the granite
   inspection table.

## DIMENSIONAL INSPECTION CRITERIA

1. Measure wall thickness at the four locations marked on the drawing — tolerance is the nominal
   value plus or minus 0.08mm unless the drawing specifies otherwise.
2. Check overall profile width and height against the go/no-go gauge; a part that fails the
   go-gauge or passes the no-go gauge is a reject.
3. Record all measurements in the QMS app against the lot traveler number — do not use paper
   backup forms except during a documented system outage.

## DISPOSITION OF NONCONFORMING MATERIAL

Any lot with a defect rate above the AQL limit is placed on Quality Hold. Tag the pallet with a
red Nonconformance tag, notify the Quality Engineer, and log the finding in the NCR system within
one shift. Do not scrap material without an approved disposition from the Quality Engineer.

## RECORD RETENTION

Inspection records are retained for 7 years per customer PPAP requirements. Records older than 90
days are archived to the document management system automatically; do not delete local tablet
records manually.

## TRAINING AND CERTIFICATION

Inspectors must complete WI-QA-014 training and pass a practical evaluation before performing
unsupervised inspection. Recertification is required annually or after any missed-defect
corrective action.

## CONTACTS

Quality Engineering office: extension 4502. For urgent hold decisions outside business hours,
contact the on-call Quality Engineer through the plant paging system.
