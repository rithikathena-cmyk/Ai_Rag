# HR Policy: Workplace Health and Safety and Harassment Reporting Policy

Document ID: HR-POL-003 | Revision: 6 | Effective Date: 2026-01-01 | Owner: Human Resources

## PURPOSE

This policy defines the company's commitment to a safe, respectful workplace free from harassment,
discrimination, and retaliation, and describes how employees can report concerns confidentially.

## SCOPE

This policy applies to all employees, contractors, and visitors at every company facility, and to
company-sponsored events and work-related communications regardless of location.

## PROHIBITED CONDUCT

Harassment based on race, color, religion, sex, national origin, age, disability, or any other
protected characteristic is strictly prohibited. This includes unwelcome verbal or physical
conduct, offensive jokes or imagery, and retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good
faith.

## REPORTING CHANNELS

1. **Direct manager:** Employees are encouraged to raise concerns with their direct manager when
   comfortable doing so.
2. **HR Business Partner:** Employees may contact their assigned HR Business Partner directly, or
   the general HR intake line at (312) 555-0164.
3. **Confidential ethics hotline:** A third-party operated hotline is available 24/7 at
   1-800-555-0182 for anonymous reporting, including anonymous web submission at
   ethics.examplecorp.com.
4. Reports may also be sent by email to hr-conduct@examplecorp.internal; this mailbox is monitored
   only by the HR Compliance team.

## INVESTIGATION PROCESS

All reports are acknowledged within 2 business days and assigned to an HR Compliance investigator.
Investigations are conducted with discretion — information is shared only with those who need to
know to conduct a thorough and fair investigation. Investigations are typically completed within
30 calendar days; complex matters may take longer, with the reporting employee updated on status
at least every 2 weeks.

## NO RETALIATION

Retaliation against an employee for reporting a concern in good faith, or for participating in an
investigation, is a violation of this policy and subject to disciplinary action up to and including
termination, regardless of the reporting employee's role or seniority.

## MANAGER RESPONSIBILITIES

Managers who receive a report, or who witness conduct that may violate this policy, must escalate
to HR within 24 hours. Managers may not attempt to independently investigate or resolve harassment
allegations.

## WORKPLACE SAFETY REPORTING

Physical safety hazards (equipment, facilities, ergonomics) should be reported through the EHS
incident portal, not the harassment reporting channels above, unless the hazard is itself the
result of intentional misconduct.
