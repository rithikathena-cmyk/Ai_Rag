# Executive Report: Q3 Enterprise Performance Summary

Document ID: EXEC-RPT-2026-Q3 | Confidentiality: Executive/Board | Owner: Office of the CEO

## OVERVIEW

This report summarizes enterprise performance across Manufacturing, HR, and Engineering for the
third quarter of fiscal year 2026, for review by the executive team and board.

## FINANCIAL HIGHLIGHTS

Consolidated revenue for Q3 was up 6.2% year-over-year, driven primarily by higher throughput on
Lines 3 through 6 following the injection-molding process improvements implemented in Q2. Gross
margin held steady at 34.1%, within 0.3 points of the annual target.

## MANUFACTURING PERFORMANCE

Overall equipment effectiveness (OEE) across the plant averaged 78%, up from 74% in Q2. The
CNC predictive-maintenance pilot on Line 7 (see Engineering's technical report ENG-TR-031)
contributed to zero unplanned spindle downtime events during the quarter. Quality escapes
(customer-reported defects per million units) fell to 42 ppm, the lowest quarterly figure in three
years.

## WORKFORCE AND HR HIGHLIGHTS

Headcount grew by 3.4% quarter-over-quarter, concentrated in Manufacturing to support the Line 4
conveyor upgrade project (PRJ-2026-014). Voluntary attrition was 8.1% annualized, below the
12-month rolling average of 9.6%. The refreshed leave and attendance policy (HR-POL-007) rolled
out in Q3 with no material increase in unexcused absences.

## ENGINEERING AND PROJECT PORTFOLIO

The Line 4 conveyor VFD upgrade remains on track for its Q2-next-year go-live, pending closure of
the PLC/VFD integration risk identified in the project's risk assessment (ENG-RA-022). The CNC
predictive maintenance pilot is recommended for expansion to all 22 machines across Lines 6–8,
pending capital approval — see ENG-TR-031 for full pilot results.

## RISK AND COMPLIANCE NOTES

No material safety incidents were reported in Q3. Two ethics hotline reports were received and
resolved within policy timelines per HR-POL-003; both were closed with no substantiated policy
violation. Enterprise risk posture is assessed as stable — see the accompanying Enterprise Risk
and Compliance Overview for the full quarterly risk register.

## OUTLOOK FOR Q4

Management expects continued margin stability, contingent on VFD equipment lead times (currently
tracking at 6 weeks) not slipping further and no disruption to the Line 4 outage window. The board
finance committee will review the Line 6–8 predictive maintenance capital request in the November
session.

## DISTRIBUTION

This report is distributed to the CEO, CFO, and board finance committee. Department-level detail
is available to Project Managers and HR leadership through their respective functional reports.
