# Executive Report: Enterprise Risk and Compliance Overview — Q3 FY2026

Document ID: EXEC-RPT-RISK-2026-Q3 | Confidentiality: Executive/Board | Owner: Office of the CEO

## PURPOSE

This overview consolidates the enterprise risk register across Manufacturing, Engineering, HR, and
Finance for executive and board review, drawing on department-level risk assessments produced this
quarter.

## TOP ENTERPRISE RISKS

1. **Supply chain / equipment lead time (Medium-High):** VFD equipment lead times for the Line 4
   conveyor upgrade are quoted at 6 weeks, close to the critical path for next year's Q2 go-live.
   Owner: Engineering. See ENG-RA-022 for the project-level mitigation plan.
2. **Single-vendor concentration (Medium):** The CNC predictive-maintenance platform used in the
   Line 7 pilot (ENG-TR-031) is single-sourced; an expansion to 22 machines would increase reliance
   on this vendor. Owner: Engineering/Procurement, to be addressed in the capital request review.
3. **Workforce attrition in skilled trades (Medium):** While overall attrition is below the
   12-month average, attrition among certified machine operators and maintenance technicians is
   trending slightly higher; HR is monitoring and will report findings alongside the Q4 workforce
   review. Owner: HR.
4. **Regulatory/compliance (Low):** No open regulatory findings at any facility. Two internal
   ethics hotline reports were closed within policy timelines in Q3 with no substantiated
   violation. Owner: HR Compliance.
5. **Cybersecurity (Low-Medium):** The new VFD drive cabinets will be networked via Modbus TCP to
   the plant PLC network; Engineering has flagged this as a segmentation review item for IT
   Security ahead of commissioning. Owner: Engineering/IT Security.

## RISK APPETITE STATEMENT

The board has affirmed a moderate risk appetite for capital projects that improve throughput or
reduce unplanned downtime, provided project-level risk assessments (such as ENG-RA-022) are
completed and mitigations tracked to closure before major outage windows are scheduled.

## GOVERNANCE AND REPORTING CADENCE

Department risk owners update their portion of the register monthly; this consolidated overview is
prepared quarterly for the board finance and audit committees. Any risk re-rated to Critical
between quarterly cycles triggers an out-of-cycle notification to the CEO and board chair within
48 hours.

## Q4 FOCUS AREAS

1. Close the PLC/VFD integration risk ahead of the Line 4 outage window.
2. Complete the IT Security segmentation review for the new VFD network connections before
   commissioning begins.
3. HR to report skilled-trades attrition trend and any proposed retention actions in the Q4
   workforce review.

## DISTRIBUTION

This overview is distributed to the CEO, CFO, board audit committee, and board finance committee.
It is not distributed below the executive level; department-specific risk items are communicated
to relevant Project Managers and HR leadership through their own functional risk assessments.
