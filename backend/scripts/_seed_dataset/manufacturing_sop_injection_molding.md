# Standard Operating Procedure: Injection Molding Machine Startup and Shutdown

Document ID: SOP-MFG-014 | Revision: 3 | Effective Date: 2026-01-15 | Owner: Manufacturing Engineering

## PURPOSE

This procedure defines the required steps for safely starting up, operating, and shutting down
Injection Molding Machines (IMM) Line 3 through Line 6 in Building B. It applies to all certified
Machine Operators and Shift Leads working in the Manufacturing department.

## SCOPE

This SOP covers Husky HyPET and Engel Duo 5550 series injection molding presses. It does not cover
blow molding equipment (see SOP-MFG-021) or extrusion lines (see SOP-MFG-009).

## SAFETY REQUIREMENTS

1. Safety glasses and heat-resistant gloves are mandatory within the yellow floor markings
   surrounding any active press.
2. No operator may bypass a mold-area light curtain or safety gate interlock for any reason. Any
   interlock fault must be reported immediately to the Shift Lead and logged in the CMMS.
3. Hot runner systems operate between 180C and 300C depending on resin. Allow a minimum 20-minute
   cooldown before any manual mold inspection.
4. In case of a hydraulic leak, hit the E-STOP, evacuate the immediate area, and notify Maintenance
   at extension 4471. Do not attempt to clean a pressurized leak.

## STARTUP PROCEDURE

1. Verify the machine logbook shows no open maintenance tickets for this press.
2. Confirm hopper resin matches the current work order — cross-check the resin lot number against
   the traveler card.
3. Power on the press controller and wait for the HMI to complete self-test (approximately 90
   seconds).
4. Bring barrel zones up to setpoint temperature gradually; do not exceed a 10C/minute ramp rate to
   avoid thermal shock to the screw.
5. Once all zones reach setpoint and hold for 5 minutes, purge the barrel per the resin-specific
   purge chart posted at each press.
6. Run 3 shots in manual mode and visually inspect for short shots, flash, or burn marks before
   switching to semi-automatic cycle.
7. Record the first-shot approval on the traveler card and notify Quality for first-piece
   inspection per WI-QA-002.

## IN-PROCESS MONITORING

- Check cycle time against the standard cycle time on the work order every hour; a deviation of
  more than 8% must be reported to the Shift Lead.
- Monitor mold temperature via the chiller readout — a rising trend of more than 5C over baseline
  indicates a possible cooling-line blockage.
- Log hopper resin level checks every 2 hours to avoid running the screw dry.

## SHUTDOWN PROCEDURE

1. Complete the current cycle and switch to manual mode — do not interrupt an in-progress
   injection stroke.
2. Retract the core and open the mold to the maintenance position.
3. Lower barrel temperatures to standby setpoint (150C) if the line will restart within 4 hours;
   otherwise perform a full purge and power down.
4. Clean the platen area of any resin drool and inspect tie bars for visible wear.
5. Complete the shift-end machine log, noting any anomalies for the incoming shift.
